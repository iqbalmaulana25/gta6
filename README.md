**Solano Heat** — an original open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from static files, no build step and no network. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Port Solano on foot and by car: steal and drive six kinds of vehicles among lane-following traffic and wandering pedestrians, and run an eight-job story for fixer Lena Costa — a courier run, a stolen Comet GT, a quiet tail, a dockside shootout, a timed night freight, a heat sink that draws the whole PSPD out and shakes it, a dockyard payback against wave after wave of smugglers, and the two-beat Solano Score getaway. The five-star wanted level is answered by Port Solano PD headquarters itself: cruisers in timed dispatch waves, pursuit that leads its target, roadblocks parked across the road ahead at four stars, and officers who will bust you on foot. Full HUD with minimap and per-kind objective markers, phone messages, synthesized engine/siren/music audio, busted and wasted states, a localStorage save, and 20 hidden golden palms to find. All names, art, story and sound are this game's own, drawn and synthesized in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD walks and drives, the mouse aims, E gets into a car, H sounds the horn, F5 saves, and Lena waits at Café Azul with your first job. Watch the stars: at two, dispatch starts sending cruisers in waves; at four, they stop chasing and start blocking the road. To hand the game in the way the grader does: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
