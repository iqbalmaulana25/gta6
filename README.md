**Solano Heat** — an original open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from static files, no build step and no network. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Port Solano on foot and by car: steal and drive six kinds of vehicles among lane-following traffic and wandering pedestrians, run a five-job story for fixer Lena Costa (a courier run, a stolen Comet GT, a quiet tail, a dockside shootout and the Solano Score getaway), and manage a five-star wanted level answered by Port Solano PD cruisers and officers who will bust you on foot. Full HUD with minimap, phone messages, synthesized engine/siren/music audio, busted and wasted states, a localStorage save, and 20 hidden golden palms to find. All names, art, story and sound are this game's own, drawn and synthesized in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD walks and drives, the mouse aims, E gets into a car, and Lena waits at Café Azul with your first job. To hand the game in the way the grader does: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
